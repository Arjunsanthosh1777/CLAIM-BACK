Local Storage, Backups & Privacy

How Your Data Is Stored

CLAIMBACK stores purchases, deadline preferences, and refund claims exclusively in your browser's localStorage. Browser-local storage is not encrypted cloud storage and can be erased if you clear your browser cache, use private browsing, or switch devices. Export a JSON backup periodically to keep a portable copy.

Safety & Consumer Policy Boundaries

Never enter bank credentials, full credit card numbers, or account passwords. Return and warranty dates calculated from user-entered durations are estimates—always verify the seller's official written return or warranty policy.
